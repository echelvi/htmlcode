What Is Mental Health First Aid?

Just like physical first aid, Mental Health First Aid equips you with the knowledge and practical skills
to recognise mental health challenges, offer initial support and help someone find the right resources.

Mental Health First Aid - ExplainedAnanya Gupta
  • 01

    Recognise the signs

    Learn how to identify signs that someone may be experiencing a mental health challenge.

  • 02

    Approach confidently

    Build practical skills for starting supportive non-judgemental conversations and responding appropriately.

  • 03

    Take the right next step

    Learn to offer initial support and connect people with professional resources when needed.

  • 04

    Enhance your self-care

    Learn to offer initial support and connect people with professional resources.

Do I need a medical or psychology background to take this course?

Not at all. MHFA is designed for everyone. No prior clinical experience is required

Is Mental Health First Aid the same as therapy or counselling?

No. Mental Health First Aid equips you with the knowledge and initial skills to recognise a mental health challenge, offer initial support and guide someone to the professional resources they need.

How is the training delivered?

Training is delivered through online live sessions and self-learning online courses.
