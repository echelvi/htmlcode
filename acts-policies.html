Supreme Court Guidelines to Safeguard Student Mental Health

The Supreme Court of India has issued guidelines emphasizing the responsibility of educational institutions to safeguard student mental health. These directions focus on prevention of student suicides, establishment of grievance redressal mechanisms, humane academic and administrative practices, access to mental health support, and the duty of care owed by institutions to students.

National Suicide Prevention Strategy (NSPS), 2022

The National Suicide Prevention Strategy, 2022 provides a comprehensive public health framework to reduce suicide mortality and morbidity in India. It emphasizes early identification, community-based interventions, responsible media reporting, restriction of access to means, crisis support systems, and coordinated action across sectors including health, education, social welfare, and law enforcement.

The National Commission for Allied and Healthcare Professions Act, 2021

An Act to provide for regulation and maintenance of standards of education and services by allied and healthcare professionals, assessment of institutions, maintenance of a Central Register and State Register and creation of a system to improve access, research and development and adoption of latest scientific advancement and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

The Prohibition of Electronic Cigarettes Act, 2019

An Act to prohibit the production, manufacture, import, export, transport, sale, distribution, storage and advertisement of electronic cigarettes in the interest of public health to protect the people from harm and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

Want to check out the entire document? Click here

The Mental Healthcare Act (MHCA), 2017

An Act to consolidate and amend the law relating to the treatment and care of mentally ill persons, to make better provision with respect to their property and affairs and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

This act repealed the Mental Health Act, 1987 and was designed in line with India’s obligations under the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (UNCRPD), aiming to shift from custodial care to a rights-based, person-centric model.

Want to check out the entire document? Click here

Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act, 2016

An Act to give effect to the United Nations Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto. This Act upholds India’s commitments under the UN Convention by ensuring legal equality, extending protections to 21 disabilities, mandating accessibility and inclusive education, and providing for reservations and penalties for rights violations.

Want to check out the entire document? Click here

National Mental Health Policy, 2014

The National Mental Health Policy 2014 outlines India’s vision for a rights-based, community-focused mental health system – emphasizing prevention, early intervention, rehabilitation, reduced stigma, and quality care across all levels. However, resource scarcity, weak coordination, and limited infrastructure have significantly delayed its realization.

The Cigarettes and Other Tobacco Products (Prohibition of Advertisement and Regulation of Trade and Commerce, Production, Supply and Distribution) Act, 2003

An Act to prohibit the advertisement of, and to provide for the regulation of trade and commerce in, and production, supply and distribution of, cigarettes and other tobacco products and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

National Trust for the Welfare of Persons with Autism, Cerebral Palsy, Mental Retardation and Multiple Disabilities Act, 1999

An act to establish a National Trust for empowering persons with autism, cerebral palsy, intellectual impairment, and other multiple disabilities – by ensuring legal guardianship where needed, fostering independent community living, and delivering welfare services through ecosystem partners.

The Cable Television Networks (Regulation) Act, 1995

An Act to regulate the operation of cable television networks in the country and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto. This Act completely prohibits advertisements that promote cigarettes and alcohol consumption (under Rule 7, Advertising Code).

The Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985

An Act to consolidate and amend the law relating to narcotic drugs, to make stringent provisions for the control and regulation of operations relating to narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, to provide for the forfeiture of property derived from, or used in, illicit traffic in narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, to implement the provisions of the International Conventions on Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances and for matters connected therewith.
