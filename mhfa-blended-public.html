MHFA Blended for Public: Online Mental Health Support Training

Built in the mould of standard Psychological First Aid (PFA) models, the MHFA Blended for Public course equips you with an evidence-based framework to identify early signs of emotional distress, manage acute mental health crises, and provide effective aid within your family, workplace, and community.

Offered by: MHFA IndiaBeginner (No prerequisites)
  • DurationApprox. 12 Hours
  • ScheduleFlexible / Self-Paced
  • Delivery100% Online or Hybrid
  • LanguageEnglish (Subtitles Available)

What you'll learn

  • Recognising the signs: Spot early behavioral, emotional, and cognitive indicators of common mental health challenges like depression and anxiety.
  • The ALGEE Framework: Master and execute the core MHFA Action Plan to evaluate, de-escalate, and guide individuals in distress.
  • Crisis First Aid: Learn safe, evidence-based steps to assist individuals dealing with suicidal thoughts, acute panic attacks, or traumatic incidents.
  • Supportive Communication: Develop non-judgmental listening techniques and communication boundaries to talk openly without causing harm.
  • Professional Care Pathways: Confidently guide individuals toward medical, clinical, and community mental health resources.
  • Caregiver Self-Preservation: Apply practical self-care routines and emotional boundaries.

Skills You Will Gain

  • Psychological First Aid Interventions
  • Active Empathetic Listening
  • Mental Health Literacy
  • Community Support Navigation
  • Personal Self-Care Strategies

How the Program Works

Designed for busy adults, students, and community leaders, this blended program pairs self-directed digital modules with real-time practical workshops.

Component 1: Self-Paced E-Learning (Foundations)

  • Format: Interactive, on-demand modules accessible on mobile, tablet, or desktop.
  • Content: Video lectures, guided readings, scenario animations, and formative knowledge checks covering common disorders, crisis definitions, and stigma-reduction strategies.

Component 2: Live Instructor-Led Workshop (Application)

  • Format: Real-time interactive session delivered online via video conference or through local in-person cohorts.
  • Content: Facilitated by an Accredited MHFA Instructor. Participants unpack realistic case studies, practice the ALGEE framework through guided role-play, and learn how to initiate sensitive conversations.
