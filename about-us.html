Mental Health First Aid Program

The Mental Health First Aid (MHFA) program was originally developed in Australia in 2000 by Betty Kitchener and Professor Anthony Jorm, a mental health researcher. Based on the research conducted with mental health professionals, advocates, and educators they designed the MHFA program focused on the core principles of providing early intervention, increasing mental health literacy, reducing stigma, and promoting early help-seeking behaviors.

The program aimed at training public members, including family members, friends, and colleagues, to recognize the signs and symptoms of common mental health problems, offer appropriate support, and guide individuals to professional help when needed.

Our story

Making a difference to our communities

MHFA India, established in 2017, operates under the license of MHFA International. It is dedicated to promoting mental health literacy, reducing stigma, and fostering positive attitudes towards early help-seeking in India.

With its headquarters in Chennai, MHFA India collaborates with Accredited Mental Health First Aid Instructors to provide training nationwide, creating a balance between mental health and physical health knowledge among the public.

Our Vision

  • Mental health knowledge and conversation are normalised in Indian society
  • Supportive actions become the natural response when someone is struggling
  • Help-seeking is encouraged
  • Everyone has the confidence and skills to support the people around them

Our Mission

  • Empower people with quality knowledge and practical skills.
  • Start a direct, compassionate conversation about suicide
  • Apply a practical action plan to keep someone safe
  • Connect a person in crisis to professional help

International Mental Health First Aid Quality Principles

The values that drive our impact across India.

Evidence into action

Grounded in rigorous evidence base that builds consensus from international research and best practices.

Mental health literacy

Aims to increase mental health literacy and the enthusiasm in individuals to take care of their own mental health.

Cultural safety

Designed to enable licensed providers to meet the unique needs of different regions and communities.

Focus on community

Focuses on community development and capacity building, expanding knowledge and confidence.

Safety-conscious

The safety and mental health of all participants are considered at every stage of delivery.

Informed by experience

Draws on feedback from instructors, participants and people with lived experience to keep improving.

Practical skill development

Focuses on practical skills, with a clear action plan to respond to developing or worsening mental health problems.

Reduce stigma & discrimination

Promotes attitudes, language and behaviour that reduce stigma and misinformation about mental health.

Inclusivity and diversity

Embraces people of all communities and supports respect for differences in race, culture, gender and ability.

Our Journey

  1. 2000

    Inception in Australia

    MHFA training was developed and delivered in Australia by Betty Kitchener and Professor Anthony Jorm.

  2. 2017

    MHFA India Licensed

    MHFA India obtained the International license to carry out the standard MHFA program across the country.

  3. 2018

    First Training Delivered

    Our first Standard MHFA training program was delivered, equipping people with practical skills to help someone they care about.

  4. 2020

    Pandemic Response

    Introduced new courses during the pandemic to deal with the emotional and mental health effects of the situation.

  5. 2021

    Trained 2000 Members During COVID-19

    Despite pandemic challenges, MHFA India trained over 2,000 individuals across corporate and public sectors, building vital mental health skills when they were needed most.
