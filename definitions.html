Mental Health Definitions

Key terms and definitions as set out in India's Mental Healthcare Act, 2017 — explained clearly, in one place.

Jump to a term

Advance Directive

Every person, who is not a minor, shall have a right to make an advance directive in writing, specifying any or all of the following, namely:

  • The way the person wishes to be cared for and treated for a mental illness.
  • The way the person wishes not to be cared for and treated for a mental illness.
  • The individual or individuals, in order of precedence, they want to appoint as their nominated representative.
Learn More

Care-giver

A person who resides with a person with mental illness and is responsible for providing care to that person, and includes a relative or any other person who performs this function, either free or with remuneration.

Learn More

Clinical Psychologist

A person:

  • Having a postgraduate degree in Psychology, Clinical Psychology or Applied Psychology, and a Master of Philosophy in Clinical Psychology or Medical and Social Psychology, obtained after a full-time course of two years including supervised clinical training from a University recognised by the University Grants Commission and approved by the Rehabilitation Council of India; or
  • Having a recognised qualification in Clinical Psychology from an institution approved by the Rehabilitation Council of India; or
  • Who imposes the least restriction on the person's rights.
Learn More

Informed Consent

Consent given for a specific intervention, without any force, undue influence, fraud, threat, mistake or misrepresentation, and obtained after disclosing adequate information — including the risks and benefits of, and alternatives to, the specific intervention — in a language and manner understood by the person.

Learn More

Insurance for Mental Illness

As per Section 21(4) of the Mental Healthcare Act, 2017, every insurer must make provision for medical insurance for the treatment of mental illness on the same basis as is available for the treatment of physical illness.

Learn More

Least Restrictive Alternative

Also known as the "least restrictive environment" or "less restrictive option." Offering an option for treatment, or a setting for treatment, which meets the person's treatment needs while placing the least possible restriction on their rights and personal liberty.

Learn More

Medical Officer in Charge

In relation to any mental health establishment, means the psychiatrist or medical practitioner who, for the time being, is in charge of that mental health establishment.

Learn More

Mental Healthcare

Includes analysis and diagnosis of a person's mental condition, and treatment, as well as care and rehabilitation of such a person for their mental illness or suspected mental illness.

Learn More

Mental Health Establishment

Any health establishment, including Ayurveda, Yoga, Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha and Homoeopathy establishments, by whatever name called, meant wholly or partly for the care of persons with mental illness — whether established, owned, controlled or maintained by the appropriate Government, local authority, trust, private or public corporation, co-operative society, organisation or any other entity — where persons with mental illness are admitted and reside for care, treatment, convalescence or rehabilitation. This also includes any general hospital or nursing home so maintained, but does not include a family residential place where a person with mental illness resides with relatives or friends.

Learn More

Mental Health Nurse

A person with a diploma or degree in general nursing, or a diploma or degree in psychiatric nursing, recognised by the Nursing Council of India and registered as such with the relevant state nursing council.

Learn More

Mental Health Professional

Means:

  • A psychiatrist; or
  • A professional registered with the concerned State Authority under Section 55; or
  • A professional holding a postgraduate degree in Mano Vigyan Avum Manas Roga (Ayurveda), Psychiatry (Homoeopathy), Moalijat Nafasiyat (Unani) or Sirappu Maruthuvam (Siddha).
Learn More

Mental Illness

A substantial disorder of thinking, mood, perception, orientation or memory that grossly impairs judgement, behaviour, and the capacity to recognise reality or meet the ordinary demands of life. This includes mental conditions associated with the abuse of alcohol and drugs, but does not include mental retardation — a condition of arrested or incomplete development of mind, specially characterised by subnormality of intelligence.

Learn More

Prisoner with Mental Illness

A person with mental illness who is an under-trial or convicted of an offence and detained in a jail or prison, and who retains the right to:

  • Appoint the individual or individuals, in order of precedence, as their nominated representative
  • Specify the way they wish not to be cared for and treated for a mental illness.
  • Specify the way they wish to be cared for and treated for a mental illness.
Learn More
