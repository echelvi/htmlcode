This Men’s Day, let’s turn silence into meaningful conversation.

Every man deserves strength, peace, and understanding — not just recognition for what he does, but support for who he is. Join us in celebrating men and boys everywhere, and take a step toward building a kinder, more mentally healthy world for all.

What should we really Mention this Men’s Day?

These seven focus areas inspire us to look beyond appreciation and focus on understanding men’s journeys — their achievements, challenges, emotions, and resilience. This Men’s Day, let’s celebrate their contributions while breaking the silence around mental health and promoting a more compassionate, supportive environment for every man.

TitleDescription
Celebrate Men’s ContributionsAcknowledge men’s roles in families, communities, workplaces, and society.

Encourage Self-Care & WellbeingPromote healthy habits such as exercise, hobbies, and mindful breaks.
Promote Support SystemsEncourage men to reach out — to friends, mentors, and professionals.
Break Stereotypes & StigmaChallenge the idea that men must always be “tough” or silent.

Driving change through our four pillars

Real change begins when awareness transforms into meaningful action. Through these four key pillars, we’re working to create a culture that values understanding, empathy, and strength — where men feel supported, informed, and empowered to care for their mental health.

PillarFocus
1. Increasing Mental Health LiteracyEmpowering men with the knowledge to recognize mental health signs and support others.
2. Reducing StigmaEncouraging open, judgment-free conversations that normalize seeking help.
3. Arming Men with Self-Management StrategiesTeaching resilience, mindfulness, and emotional regulation skills.
4. Supporting Access to Mental Health ServicesConnecting men to timely, confidential, and stigma-free care.

Join the Men’s November Campaign — Celebrate, Speak, and Support.

This November, be part of the #WhatToMENtion movement — a campaign that celebrates men for who they are and opens up real conversations about mental health. Together, we can build awareness, foster empathy, and create a culture where every man feels seen, supported, and understood.

Ways to Participate:

  • Post a Story: Share one positive thing you’d like to MENtion about the men who inspire you — colleagues, friends, or family.
  • Create a Short Video: Record a 30-second video on what men’s mental health means to you and why it matters.
  • Tag & Nominate: Tag a man who inspires you and include #WhatToMENtion in your post to keep the message going.
  • Use Our Collaterals: Download and share campaign visuals, templates, and social media assets to spread awareness within your network.

After completing the weekly tasks, make use of the downloadable collateral to share your success. Don't forget to tag #MHFAINDIA on social media to spread the word and inspire others to join the movement!
