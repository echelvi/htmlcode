MHFA Blended for Workplace: Corporate Mental Health Training & Employee Wellness Programs

The MHFA Blended for Workplace course equips your workforce with an evidence-based framework to spot early warning signs, deliver initial support through the standard MHFA Action Plan, and build sustained employee wellbeing across in-office and remote teams.

Offered by: MHFA IndiaBeginner to Intermediate
  • DurationApprox. 12 Hours
  • ScheduleFlexible / Self-Paced
  • Delivery100% Online or Hybrid
  • LanguageEnglish (Subtitles Available)

What you'll learn

  • Early Detection: Spot emerging behavioural and cognitive signs of poor mental health across corporate environments.
  • The MHFA Action Plan: Apply the proven, structured five-step framework to support colleagues experiencing distress or crisis.
  • Empathetic Communication: Master non-judgmental active listening, validation techniques, and de-escalation skills.
  • Ethics & Safeguards: Conduct supportive one-on-one interventions with healthy professional boundaries and strict confidentiality.
  • Crisis Management: Safely address crisis situations, including acute anxiety episodes, substance misuse emergencies, and suicidal distress.
  • Peer Wellbeing: Integrate proactive self-care protocols and peer-support habits to prevent caregiver burnout.

Skills You Will Gain

  • Crisis Intervention & De-escalation
  • Psychological First Aid (Workplace Context)
  • Active Listening & Empathic Dialogue
  • Mental Health Literacy
  • Workplace Wellness Advocacy
  • Professional Boundary Setting

Course Format & Learning Experience

This program is structured into two complementary components designed to fit the schedules of working professionals.

Component 1: Self-Paced E-Learning

  • Focus: Foundational mental health literacy, diagnostic awareness, and core frameworks.
  • Experience: Interactive digital modules, self-reflection prompts, video explainers, and self-checks covering anxiety, depression, psychosis, and substance use.

Component 2: Instructor-Led Workshop

  • Focus: Practical application, role-playing, and scenario-based interventions.
  • Experience: Live virtual or on-premise session led by an Accredited MHFA Instructor. Participants unpack real-world workplace case studies, rehearse difficult conversations, and practice the MHFA Action Plan in a guided group environment.
